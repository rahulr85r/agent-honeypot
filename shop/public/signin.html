Questions from new partners

Why can't I find you on review sites?

We only sell to onboarded business partners through this portal, so there is no public storefront to review. References are available from your account manager on request.

What do the grades mean?

Grade A: light signs of use, screen flawless. Grade B: visible marks on the frame or back, screen flawless. Every grade is fully functional with battery health of 80% or more.

Are the devices new?

No. They are discontinued models, refurbished and graded. That is the point: they match the hardware and software your long-tail users actually run.

Can I return a unit?

Yes. Any unit that fails functionally within 90 days is replaced or credited back to your account.
